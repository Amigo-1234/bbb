Who We Are

BIA JUPEB (Bomas Intellectual Academy) is a leading JUPEB preparation academy in Nigeria, dedicated to helping students secure direct entry admission into 200 level in top universities without writing JAMB.

We provide both physical classes in Lagos and a powerful online learning system that allows students across Nigeria to study from anywhere and still succeed.

What We Offer

  • ✔ Complete JUPEB preparation (Science & Non-Science)
  • ✔ Online classes with recorded + live lessons
  • ✔ Physical classes in Lagos (Bariga centre)
  • ✔ Continuous assessments & exam practice
  • ✔ Admission guidance into universities

Our Vision

To become the most trusted JUPEB academy in Nigeria by providing quality education, mentorship, and guaranteed academic success.

Our Mission

To help every student gain admission into their dream university through structured learning, discipline, and modern teaching methods.
